About Kumomacha

Kumomacha is a premium brand of Japanese matcha tea. We bring you carefully selected, organic ceremonial-grade matcha that’s smooth, vibrant, and packed with wellness benefits.

Our Mission

Our mission is to introduce the traditional art of matcha to a modern world. We believe in sustainability, mindfulness, and the joy of authentic taste. Every cup of Kumomacha is an invitation to slow down and enjoy life.

Why Choose Us?

  • Rich in Antioxidants and Amino Acids
  • Supports Energy, Focus, and Calmness
  • Ideal for lattes, smoothies, and desserts
